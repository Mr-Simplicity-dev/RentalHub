# RentalHub NG premium advert source

A self-contained, vertical 30-second advert for RentalHub NG. It speaks plainly to Nigerian renters without making unverified claims such as “verified listings,” “zero agency fee,” or “instant approval.”

## Story

| Time | Message | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7s | House hunting should not feel like guesswork. | Premium navy opening and search prompt |
| 7–15s | Search homes. Make your move. | Stylised RentalHub property-search interface |
| 15–23s | Less stress. More clarity. | Three concise benefits |
| 23–30s | Find it with RentalHub NG. | Clean CTA and deliberate final hold |

## Render instructions

1. Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge at 100% zoom.
2. Record the browser tab at **1080 × 1920, 30 fps** for exactly **30 seconds**. Do not crop or speed up the clip.
3. Export H.264 MP4 at 12–20 Mbps. Use a quiet, warm Afrobeats-inspired instrumental at low volume; do not use copyrighted music without a licence.
4. Add a Nigerian English voice-over, calm and confident, following the on-screen copy. Keep the final screen visible for the full seven seconds.

The project is intentionally self-contained so it will still open without image, stock-video, or font assets. For a final paid campaign, replace the illustrated property card with licensed footage or a real high-quality RentalHub screen recording, after removing personal data.
