# RentalHub NG account-creation campaign

This folder contains the approved creative direction for two separate, 25-second vertical adverts.

* `tenant-ad.md` — for people looking for a home.
* `landlord-ad.md` — for people listing or managing property.
* `capture-manifest.md` — the exact real screens needed from Android, web, and iOS.

Do not substitute mock screens for capture files in `captures/`. The finished advert should use only rendered RentalHub screens populated with the fictional demo account **Adam Hawa**.

## Output

Create a master MP4 at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 25 seconds. From the master, create 1080 × 1080 and 1920 × 1080 cut-downs; do not simply crop the vertical edit.

## Voice and caption treatment

Use a warm Nigerian-English voice, restrained Afrobeats-inspired licensed music, and on-screen captions matching the narration. Keep music under the voice and keep the final call-to-action visible for at least four seconds.
